Browser data access

When you ask an agent to perform a browser task, Equinox Browser may send tab content, URLs, screenshots, text you enter, and when needed limited console/network metadata to your local Equinox Local runtime. The extension also has Chrome's bookmarks permission so agents can manage Agent Browser bookmarks inside the isolated Agent Browser profile; Equinox Local intentionally blocks automation access to Your Browser bookmarks. The task-relevant data may also be sent to the AI service you choose to complete that request. This data is used only to perform the browser action you requested.

Browser automation stays off until you press the button below.
