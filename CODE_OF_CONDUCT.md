# Code of Conduct

Equinox Local aims to be a technically rigorous and welcoming open-source project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique code, design, evidence, and trade-offs rather than the person presenting them. Assume good faith while still asking for clear technical justification when a change affects reliability or security.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- providing reproducible bug reports;
- explaining disagreement with concrete technical reasoning;
- helping contributors understand an unfamiliar part of the codebase;
- acknowledging uncertainty or changing your mind when evidence changes; and
- keeping reviews focused on the contribution rather than unrelated personal characteristics.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, targeted insults, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publication of another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Security findings must not be used to expose user data, credentials, or unrelated private project contents. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for responsible reporting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated abuse may result in a ban from project spaces.

Concerns about conduct can be sent privately to **iletisim@sametbasbug.dev**.
